Zain E-sports Marvel Rivals Tournament

Date
February 21-23, 2025
Location
Zain E-sports Lab, Zain Tower

The Zain E-sports Marvel Rivals Tournament was a landmark event, bringing together 13 teams for a 6v6 showdown in one of the most anticipated new games. Hosted at the Zain E-sports Lab, the tournament featured professional production, live streaming, and a collaborative effort between Zain E-sports, Teal Flamingo, and BEC.

Tournament Overview

This Marvel Rivals tournament provided a platform for top teams to compete in a high-energy, professionally managed environment. The event was streamed live on Twitch and featured a bracket managed on Challonge.

Format: 6v6 Marvel Rivals
Bracket: Single Elimination
Teams: 16
Platform: Challonge for brackets, Twitch for streaming
