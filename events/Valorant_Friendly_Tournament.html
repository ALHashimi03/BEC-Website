About the Event

Hosted at Darkness Gaming Center in A'ali, the Valorant Friendly Tournament brought together players from across Bahrain's gaming community for a competitive 5v5 showdown. The event was delivered through a collaboration between Darkness Gaming Center and the Bahrain Esports Community, providing participants with a professional and enjoyable tournament experience.

Tournament Format

  • Valorant 5v5 tournament
  • 8 participating teams
  • Single-elimination bracket
  • LAN competition hosted at Darkness Gaming Center
  • Community-focused competitive environment
  • Live tournament coverage and event highlights

BEC's Contribution

  • Tournament administration and operations
  • Refereeing and match moderation
  • Live streaming and broadcast support
  • Event graphics and marketing
  • Coordination with Darkness Gaming Center
  • Community engagement and player support

Final Standings

Event Highlights

The collaboration between Darkness Gaming Center and BEC successfully brought together eight teams and forty players in a competitive yet welcoming esports environment. Through tournament operations, streaming, and community engagement, the event showcased the continued growth of Bahrain's Valorant scene.
