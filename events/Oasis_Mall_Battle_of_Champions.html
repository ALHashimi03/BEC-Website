About the Event

The Oasis Mall Battle of Champions brought football and gaming together at Oasis Mall in Juffair with an EA FC 26 tournament held from July 2-4, 2026. The tournament served as the finale of a 17-day Oasis Malls Battle of Champions Football Activation, capping off more than two weeks of football-themed entertainment with a competitive gaming showdown that drew players, families, and members of Bahrain's esports community.

Tournament Format

  • EA FC 26 competitive tournament
  • Held over three days at Oasis Mall, Juffair
  • Open to football and gaming enthusiasts of all levels
  • Finale event of the 17-day Battle of Champions activation

Prize Pool

  • 🥇 1st Place: BD 100
  • 🥈 2nd Place: BD 75
  • 🥉 3rd Place: BD 50

BEC's Contribution

The event was organized by Media Circuit on behalf of Oasis Malls, with the Bahrain Esports Community (BEC) supporting as a key partner.

  • Esports partnership and tournament support
  • Community engagement and player outreach
  • Collaboration with Media Circuit on event delivery

Event Highlights

The EA FC 26 Tournament brought the Oasis Malls Battle of Champions activation to a memorable close, uniting football fans, gamers, and the local esports community for a finale that celebrated 17 days of community entertainment. The collaboration between Media Circuit and BEC delivered an engaging experience that highlighted the growing overlap between football culture and competitive gaming in Bahrain.
