About the Event

The Toyota GR e-Racing Championship took over Bahrain City Centre from September 1-5, 2026, inviting racers of all levels to set their fastest lap and compete for a share of BD 1,800 in cash prizes. Open registration ran directly at the Toyota stand, giving walk-in visitors and dedicated competitors alike a shot at a Top 16 qualifying spot and a place in the bracket that followed.

Format & Schedule

  • 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM: Free training and practice sessions on the simulators
  • 4:00 PM - 10:00 PM: Official qualifying window, racers set a timed lap to compete for a Top 16 spot
  • Qualifiers progressed into a group-based bracket: Quarterfinals, Semifinals, and a Grand Final
  • Registration available on-site at the Toyota stand throughout the event

Daily Turnout

Turnout grew steadily across the qualifying days, with strong walk-in participation alongside registered competitors:

  • Day 1: 50 registered participants, plus roughly 100-150 free-entry visitors
  • Day 2: 41 new registered participants (91 total), plus roughly 175-200 free-entry visitors, with many Day 1 racers returning to improve their times
  • Day 3: 111 new registered participants (202 total across the event), plus roughly 50 free-entry visitors. Demand was high enough that re-entry had to pause around 9:00 PM and new sign-ups closed around 9:30 PM as the simulators reached capacity, with peak wait times of about 30 minutes

Event Highlights

Over five days at Bahrain City Centre, the Toyota GR e-Racing Championship drew more than 200 registered racers and hundreds more free-entry visitors to the simulators, with demand peaking on Day 3 as queues and wait times grew throughout the evening. From the Quarterfinal groups through to a tightly contested Grand Final, Omar Qaed came out on top to claim the championship, with Hasan Khalil, Ebrahim Khalid, and Saeed Sahwan rounding out the Top 4.
