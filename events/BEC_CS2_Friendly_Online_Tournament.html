About the Event

The BEC CS2 Friendly Online Tournament was organized by the Bahrain Esports Community as a community-focused Counter-Strike 2 competition. Hosted online through Discord, the event provided players with an opportunity to compete, connect, and showcase their skills in a structured tournament environment.

Tournament Format

  • Counter-Strike 2 (CS2)
  • 5v5 team format
  • Single-elimination bracket
  • Best-of-1 matches
  • Online tournament hosted via Discord
  • Live coverage through Twitch and social media highlights
