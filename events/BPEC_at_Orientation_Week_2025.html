Bahrain Polytechnic Esports Club at Orientation Week 2025

September 7–9, 2025Bahrain Polytechnic, Campus A – Hall 12
3Days
1Main Zone
600+Attendees

The Bahrain Polytechnic Esports Club (BPEC) with collaborations between Bahrain Esports Community (BEC) and DARKNESS Gaming Center proudly joined Orientation Week 2025, welcoming new and returning students to explore the exciting world of gaming and esports at Bahrain Polytechnic.

The event served as a gateway for students to connect with the BPEC community, experience live gaming setups, and learn about upcoming tournaments, events, and club opportunities for the new academic year.

Whether you’re a competitive player, a casual gamer, or simply curious about esports, BPEC’s booth at Orientation Week offered the perfect chance to join the Polytechnic’s growing esports family.

Event Details

Venue: Bahrain Polytechnic, Hall 12
Dates: September 7–9, 2025
Type: Campus Exhibition & Gaming Showcase

Event Highlights

  • Esports booth with multiple game setups
  • BPEC team meet & greet sessions
  • club registration
  • Live demonstrations and gameplay
  • Community engagement and networking opportunities

BEC's Role & Responsibilities

The Bahrain Esports Community (BEC) collaborated with the Bahrain Polytechnic Esports Club to support the event setup and enhance the overall experience.

  • Booth and gaming station setup
  • Technical support and equipment
  • Community engagement and promotion
  • Photography and social media coverage

Impact & Future

BPEC’s presence at Orientation Week 2025 successfully introduced dozens of new students to the esports culture at Bahrain Polytechnic.

This event marked the start of a new semester filled with gaming, competition, and community, reinforcing Polytechnic’s position as a hub for esports growth and student engagement.

As the club expands, future plans include campus tournaments, workshops, and partnerships that will continue to strengthen Bahrain’s collegiate esports ecosystem.
