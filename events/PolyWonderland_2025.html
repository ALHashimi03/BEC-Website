About the Event

PolyWonderland 2025 is Bahrain Polytechnic's annual student festival, bringing together students, clubs, and visitors through a variety of activities and entertainment. As part of the event, the Bahrain Esports Community (BEC) collaborated with Bahrain Polytechnic Esports Club (BPEC) to support the gaming and esports experience offered to attendees.

Gaming & Entertainment Zones

  • Virtual Reality (VR) experiences
  • High-performance PC gaming area
  • PlayStation 5 gaming stations
  • Racing simulator experience
  • Retro gaming corner
  • Nintendo gaming zone
  • Community art wall

Event Highlights

Through its partnership with BPEC, BEC helped ensure the successful delivery of the gaming and esports activities at PolyWonderland 2025. The collaboration showcased the strength of Bahrain's esports community and demonstrated the value of supporting student-led initiatives and events.
